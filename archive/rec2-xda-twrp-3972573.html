    Team Win Recovery Project 3.x, or twrp3 for short, is a custom recovery built with ease of use and customization in mind. It's a fully touch driven user interface no more volume rocker or power buttons to mash. The GUI is also fully XML driven and completely theme-able. You can change just about every aspect of the look and feel.

    Features:
    All function in a normal TWRP
    Supports English and Chinese
    Supports MTP

    Install guide:
    1. Install fastboot tools from google's sdk on your PC
    2. Enter fastboot mode
    3. Unlock your bootloader
    4. Run "fastboot flash recovery recovery.img" in command line
    5. Reboot and enjoy it

      HOW TO FLASH TWRP FOR THE FIRST TIME

      Bootloader should be unlocked before going into these steps


      Install minimal adb & fastboot tools on your PC

      1. Copy twrp.img file into minimal adb & fastboot folder...also into your phone's Sdcard.

      2. Goto fastboot mode by long pressing volumeUp+PowerButton.
      Phone boots into fastboot mode. Now Connect your phone to pc.

      3. Open cmd/powershell in minimal adb& fastboot folder.

      4. Now in the cmd type fastboot boot twrp.img

      Now your phone boots into TWRP.

      5. From booted TWRP, goto Wipe>FormatData>type YES.

      6. Now goto Install and select your twrp.img from your sdcard and flash it targeting RECOVERY.

      7. Goto Reboot>Click recovery.
      Your phone again boots into TWRP.
      Now you have TWRP installed.

      DONT REBOOT INTO SYSTEM NOW ELSE YOUR TWRP WLL BE REPLACED BY STOCK RECOVERY.

      8. To keep twrp forever, Flash disable-force-encryption-treble zip file.
      Above file also installs magisk. So, if you dont want root, flash magisk uninstaller next after flashing above file.

      9. Finally reboot into system.

      NOTE: do it at your own risk.
      Flash latest fastboot rom of your device if something goes wrong.

      I finally did it and then i saw your explenation, but at step 2 you need to pres volume - to get into fastboot

      What i did:

      Step 1: adb and fastboot, and I placed the recovery.img in the folder
      Step 2: Rebooted phone into fastboot (can done with volume - and power) or with adb command: "adb reboot bootloader" when phone is android.
      Step 3a: I typed the command: fastboot flash recovery recovery.img (to install recovery)
      Step 3b: I typed the command: fastboot boot recovery.img (to boot in recovery)
      I did both these just to make sure, but perhaps just 1 of the above works as well)

      Step 4: fastboot reboot recovery (if you only choose step3a) , with step 3b it will reboot a few seconds after enter.
      Important: When the phone reboots, press now the volume + button, this is to get into recovery!

      After this i finally hard twrp recovery.

      Step 5: Install in twrp again the twrp recovery (recovery.img) onto the recovery partitition (to keep it forever, i mounted the phone to place it on the internal disk)
      Step 5B: reboot into recovery (and it did)
      Step 6: Install magisk 19.3 (for root)
      Step 7: Reboot into system and I was rooted!

      Some guides tell to format data, I didn't because I didn't want to set EVERYTHING up again after losing it with unlocking bootloader
      But everything works, if I will run into issues I will update this message.
      I also didn't install the: disable-force-encryption-treble zip file. because in telegram groups and other guides they also don't talk about it.
