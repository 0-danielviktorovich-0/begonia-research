## 2. Takedown / correction policy

If you are the author of quoted or archived material and object to its
inclusion in any form — open an issue in this repository or contact the
repository owner, and the item will be **removed or reduced to a short
attributed citation with a link to your original** within days, no questions
asked. Correction requests (wrong attribution, updated links) are handled the
same way.
